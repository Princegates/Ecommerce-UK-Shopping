# 3. Customer guide

Written for shoppers, in plain language. Staff can copy any part of it into a help page, a WhatsApp reply or a message to a customer.

## What this shop does

You choose items from UK shops. You pay us **once, in Ghana cedis**. We buy the items in the UK, ship them to Ghana, and a courier brings them to your address. You can follow every step.

The price you see includes the items, our service charge, shipping to Ghana and delivery to your address. **Customs duty or taxes charged in Ghana, if any, are not included.**

## Finding something

- **Browse**: the shop pages, the departments (Fashion, Electronics and so on) and the **Browse** menu at the top.
- **Search**: type in the search bar. Suggestions appear as you type; filters and sorting are on the results page.
- **Today's deals**: items with a lower price for a limited time.
- **Prices**: every price is shown in **pounds** (the UK price) and **cedis** (what you pay). On a product page, the **"to your door"** box shows the full cost for your delivery area.
- **Saved items**: tap the heart to save something for later (you need to be signed in).

## Can't find it here? Paste the link

You can buy from **almost any UK website**, even if it is not listed here.

1. Open the product on the UK shop's website and choose your size and colour.
2. Copy the web address from your browser.
3. Paste it into the box on our home page (or the **Add by link** button at the top, the search bar, or the **Paste link** tab on a phone).
4. We show the price if we can read it. Choose the quantity, the kind of item and your size or colour, then tap **Get my full price and pay**.
5. If we cannot price it straight away, we check it for you and **send you a message** with your price and a link to pay. It also appears in your account under **Your link requests**.

The price is held for a few days. After that it may have changed, so ask us for a fresh one.

## Ordering

1. **Add to cart** (choose your size and colour first where there is a choice).
2. **Checkout**. You need an account: your name, a phone number we can reach you on, and a password.
3. Choose your **delivery area** and **shipping method** (for example Air freight or Express air). The total updates as you choose.
4. Enter your delivery address and a landmark or GhanaPost GPS code if you have one.
5. Choose how you want **updates**: SMS, email and/or WhatsApp.
6. **Place order and pay.** You are sent to the payment page of our payment partner. We never see or store your card details.

There is a **minimum order** (items only). The cart tells you how close you are.

## Paying

Payment options depend on what the shop has switched on, for example Mobile Money and cards through Paystack or Flutterwave, or international cards through Stripe. Your order is only bought **after payment is confirmed**. If a payment fails you can try again from your account.

## Tracking your order

Open **Account → Orders**, or use **Track an order** with your order number and the phone number or email you used.

Your order moves through these steps:

| Step | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting payment | We start buying as soon as your payment is confirmed. |
| Payment received | Your payment is confirmed and your items are queued for buying. |
| Buying from the UK shop | Our team is placing your order with the UK shop. |
| Bought from the UK shop | The UK shop has accepted the order and will send it to us. |
| Received at our UK address | Your parcel has reached our UK address and is being prepared for export. |
| On its way to Ghana | Your parcel has left the UK. |
| Clearing customs in Ghana | Your parcel has arrived in Ghana and is being cleared. |
| Out for delivery | A rider is bringing your order to your address. |
| Delivered | Your order has been delivered. |

Where we have a carrier and reference for a stage you will see them, often with a link. **Account → Updates** is a feed of everything that has changed.

## Cancelling and refunds

You can ask us to cancel **before we have bought your items** (up to the *Buying from the UK shop* step). Once an item is bought from the UK shop it usually cannot be cancelled. If you paid and the order is cancelled, we refund you and mark the order **Refunded**; the time to reach your account depends on your payment method. Contact us to ask.

## Your account

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your latest order and its tracking, quick counts, your link requests and "Buy again". |
| **Orders** | Every order, with tracking. |
| **Updates** | A feed of changes to your orders. |
| **Saved items** | Your wishlist. |
| **Addresses** | Up to 10 saved delivery addresses. |
| **Profile** | Your name, phone, email and how you want to be notified. |
| **Security** | Change your password, sign out other devices, or delete your account. |

**Forgotten password?** Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. We send a link that works for 60 minutes. If you cannot receive it, contact us and we can create a one-time link for you.

## Reviews

Once an item is **delivered** you can review it, once. Your review shows your first name and the initial of your last name (for example "Ama M."). We may hide reviews that break our rules.

## Staying safe

- We will **never ask for your password** or card number by message, phone or WhatsApp.
- Pay only on the payment page you reach from our site.
- Messages from us come from our shop's name; if in doubt, sign in to your account and check your orders there.

## Getting help

Use the contact details shown on the site (for example the WhatsApp number in the footer) and quote your **order number**.
